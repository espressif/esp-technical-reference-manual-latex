\regfield{LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_CNT\_CLR}{1}{28}{{0}}%
\regfield{LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_RESET\_WAIT}{10}{18}{{0x3ff}}%
\regfield{LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_INTR\_WAIT}{10}{8}{{1}}%

\begin{regdesc}\begin{reglist}
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0CLOSEFLASHENA}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_CLOSE\_FLASH\_ENA] Configures whether to enable the brown-out detector to trigger flash suspend. \\
0: Disable \\
1: Enable\\
(R/W)
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0PDRFENA}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_PD\_RF\_ENA]  Configures whether to enable the brown-out detector to power down the RF module. \\
0: Disable \\
1: Enable\\
(R/W)
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0INTRWAIT}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_INTR\_WAIT] Configures the time to generate an interrupt after the brown-out signal is valid. The unit is LP\_FAST\_CLK cycles. (R/W)
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0RESETWAIT}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_RESET\_WAIT] Configures the time to generate a reset after the brown-out signal is valid. The unit is LP\_FAST\_CLK cycles. (R/W)
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0CNTCLR}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_CNT\_CLR] Configures whether to clear the count value of the brown-out detector.\\
0: Do not clear\\
1: Clear\\
(R/W)
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0INTRENA}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_INTR\_ENA] Enables the interrupts for the brown-out detector mode 0. \hyperref[fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0INTRAW]{LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_INT\_RAW} and \hyperref[fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0LPINTRAW]{LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_LP\_INT\_RAW} are valid only when this field is set to 1.\\
0: Disable \\
1: Enable\\
(R/W)
\label{fielddesc:LPANABODMODE0RESETSEL}\item [LP\_ANA\_BOD\_MODE0\_RESET\_SEL] Configures the reset type when the brown-out detector is triggered.\\
0: Chip reset\\
1: System reset\\
(R/W)
